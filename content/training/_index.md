  Two live hours each week, usually on Monday and Tuesday nights, and one
  ongoing lab in a managed environment.

  Every lab is written so that you can still do it on your own if you miss
  a session. A lot of them are built to fail. At some point the cluster will
  refuse a step, and figuring out why is half the lesson.

  No homework and no grades. Each week ends with extra reading if you want
  it, and nothing later in the course depends on it.
