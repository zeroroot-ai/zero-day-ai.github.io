  For twenty years the deal was simple. Pick a lane. Be the network person,
  the security person, the Kubernetes person, the model person. Go deep, stay
  there, get paid.

  AI ended that deal. It did not replace you. It let one person do the work
  of a whole row of lanes. The person who can see across all of them is now
  worth ten who cannot.

  So the job changed. You have to be able to break the thing. Then you have
  to know exactly why it broke. Then you close it, so the same break never
  works again. Then you ship it, on real hardware, to real users, and you
  stand behind it.

  Break it. Secure it. Ship it. **That is one skill now, not three careers.**

  It is the only thing we teach.
