## The party

Hack The Planet LLC sells the seats and signs the assessment and build
contracts. Zero Day AI Labs is the name it trades under on this site.

## A seat

A seat is one place in one course for one cohort. It includes every live
session of that course, every lab, the recordings of every session, a tenant
on the lab cluster for the length of the cohort plus one week, and the
private cohort channel on Discord. A seat is for one person. A team seat is
the same thing bought in a block.

## The refund rule

A seat is refunded in full, for any reason, until the first live session of
the cohort starts. After that session there is no refund. Write to
class@zero-day.ai before the first session and the refund goes back to the
card that paid.

## What you keep

Everything you build in a course is yours. The course repository, the
manifests, and the code you write stay with you. The recordings are for your
own use and are not for redistribution.

## Changes to a cohort

If we move a cohort's start date, every buyer hears first and may take the
full refund instead. If we cancel a cohort, every seat is refunded in full.

## Questions

Write to class@zero-day.ai about a seat you hold, or to info@zero-day.ai
about anything else.
