This page says what the site holds about you. It is short because the site
holds little.

## The list

When you put an address in a form on this site, it goes to the list on
beehiiv. beehiiv stores the address, the date, and which form it came from:
training or consulting. We use it to send you the newsletter, the cohort
dates, and the payment link. We do not sell it and we do not share it. Every
mail carries an unsubscribe link, and that link removes you.

## Analytics

The site asks before it sets a cookie. If you accept, Google Analytics
records the pages you read and the page you left from. If you decline, no
analytics cookie is set and nothing is recorded. The choice is remembered in
your browser and you can change it there.

## Payment

A seat is paid through Stripe. Stripe holds the card and the billing
details. We see the name, the email address, the course, and the amount. We
never see the card number.

## Mail

If you write to info@zero-day.ai, the mail sits in a Google Workspace
mailbox that a person reads. Replies come from the same mailbox or from
class@zero-day.ai.

## Removal

Write to info@zero-day.ai and ask. We remove you from the list and delete
the mail thread. Payment records stay as long as the law requires a business
to keep them.

## Who holds this

Hack The Planet LLC runs this site and holds the data above.
